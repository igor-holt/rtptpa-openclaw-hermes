# Power-Tower Fleet

## Overview

Reusable primitive for loosely coupled Grok agents that compound without collapsing into noise. The fleet is not a marketing voice. It is a stacking process: community primitives plus real leverage, timed against invariants that do not move.

## When to Activate

- Direct: power-tower-fleet, tower-orchestrator, community-accumulator, leverage-accelerator, invariant-monitor, yield-timer, power-arbiter, fleet-scribe.
- Automatic: Genesis Conductor UCP power-tower arbitration, RTPTPA handoff, multi-agent compounding questions, yield-versus-height timing decisions.

| bot_id | role | speaks when |

| invariant-monitor | constraint-watcher | naming what still binds |
| yield-timer | opportunist | harvest / reinvest / leave unclaimed |
| power-arbiter | final-decider | binding-invariant judgment when the swarm disagrees |

## Core Principles

- Prefer reusable primitives over one-off answers.
- Reject fake leverage: more parallelism that raises coordination tax is not acceleration.
- Harvest too early and the tower caps. Wait too long and the priced invariant has moved.
- Decide by the binding invariant, not by volume.
- Do not flatten the claim into hype.
