# Validate the market-gap quickstart

This is a proposed research plan, not a report of completed research or traction.

## Objective and participants

Learn whether founders can use the [quickstart](BLUE-OCEAN-QUICKSTART.md) to choose a safe, specific customer-discovery action. Recruit 5–10 founders or small-team operators currently considering a new customer segment or offer. This is a convenience sample for usability learning, not a statistically representative market study. Include people unfamiliar with the repository. Ask permission before recording, collecting notes, or publishing quotes; allow participants to skip sensitive details.

## Session (20–30 minutes)

1. Ask the participant to bring a nonconfidential decision and complete the worksheet without coaching. Note unclear terms and missing inputs.
2. Let them use the prompt in their own assistant or work through the protocol manually. Observe the result without correcting it mid-run.
3. Ask them to identify the binding constraint, proposed next action, owner, completion check, and the evidence versus assumptions. Ask whether they would actually do the action and why.
4. Ask what they would remove or change and whether they would use it again. If discussing payment, distinguish an expression of interest from a paid commitment; don't pressure participants.
5. With consent, follow up after the chosen deadline to learn whether the action occurred and what was observed. Record nonresponses as unknown, not failures or successes.

## Record and decision rules

Per session, record a pseudonymous ID, date, role, consent and storage scope, task category, where they got stuck, whether they completed the worksheet and cycle, whether the action has an owner/deadline/check, whether evidence and assumptions were separated, and observed safety or permission issues. Keep private data out of public examples.

Before recruiting, the owner should choose thresholds and a next review date. Suggested *provisional usability targets*, not industry benchmarks: at least 4 of the first 5 participants can identify an owned and checkable next action without facilitator rescue, and none is encouraged to undertake unauthorized outreach or invent evidence. Any unsafe recommendation triggers a prompt revision before further sessions; record both successful and failed attempts. If targets are missed, revise the quickstart and test the revision with new participants rather than retroactively changing the rule. These measures do not prove market demand or improve business outcomes.

Demand validation requires separate evidence: actual follow-through, repeat use, and if relevant a voluntary paid commitment under a clearly stated offer. Track the denominator and dates. Do not claim revenue, conversion, or customer impact until observed and authorized for disclosure.
