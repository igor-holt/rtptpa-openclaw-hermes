# Internal pilot: choosing the next validation step

**Status:** desk exercise using repository contents, not independent research. No customers were contacted and no demand, sale, or effectiveness was measured. This is not a substitute for testing with participants.

## Input

- **Decision:** What should the owner do next to learn whether the market-gap quickstart is useful to founders?
- **Owner:** repository operator; no named person or deadline was provided.
- **Customer hypothesis:** founders and small teams exploring a new segment may want help choosing their next discovery action. Unverified.
- **Current alternatives:** informal founder judgment, customer-discovery worksheets, advisors, and generic AI prompts are plausible alternatives; specific comparisons have not been researched.
- **Evidence:** this repository contains a protocol and quickstart; the [validation plan](VALIDATION-PLAN.md) proposes user sessions. It does not document completed sessions, demand, or sales.
- **Constraints:** external outreach, storage of participant data, spending, and publication require the operator's authorization. Budget and access to participants are unknown. `risk_R` is unknown.

## Cycle

1. **Frame:** An artifact exists, but whether a founder can use it unaided is unknown. The binding constraint is the absence of independent usability observations. Forbidden: contacting participants or recording private data without permission.
2. **Compound:** A repeatable session script and empty log are reusable assets that could lower the effort of collecting comparable observations; this is a hypothesis until used. Further branding is additive until it resolves an observed need.
3. **Intervene:** Prepare a recruitment and usability-testing kit; defer a paid offer or launch until there is evidence to shape it.
4. **Surplus:** No user benefit or willingness to pay has been measured. The kit may duplicate standard research practices, so its incremental commercial benefit is unverified: `none` demonstrated. Authorization and privacy constraints remain binding.
5. **Timing:** `reinvest` in research preparation; do not claim the market is uncontested or commit to a product launch.
6. **Decision:** Retain the quickstart and existing protocol; defer claims of traction and payment. Unknowns include participant availability, actual usability, and follow-through.
7. **Summary:** The quickstart exists but has no documented independent usability tests. The binding constraint is lack of observations from its intended audience. **Next vector:** the repository operator, at a date they choose, reviews [the research kit](RESEARCH-KIT.md) and decides whether to authorize the first five sessions; completion is an explicit recorded approval or rejection, not a claim that recruitment occurred.

## Provisional discovery hypothesis

Founders exploring a new customer segment may value a compact process for selecting a next customer-discovery action over their existing ad hoc approach because they may be uncertain which assumption to test first. All elements are unverified. With approval, the first five usability sessions can test whether people can finish the cycle unaided and identify an owned, checkable action; the [validation plan](VALIDATION-PLAN.md) specifies provisional criteria. This tests usability, not willingness to pay or market uniqueness.
