# Launch and outreach copy

This is an early, research-oriented release of a decision aid, not a claim of validated market demand. Adapt only after reviewing accuracy and permissions. No endorsements are implied.

## Public announcement

> Exploring a new customer segment? We made a free, open repository worksheet for choosing one customer-discovery action: define the customer and current alternatives, separate evidence from assumptions, and leave with an owner and a checkable next step. It includes a copy-paste assistant prompt and a manual option. It's an early prototype, not proof that a market is uncontested or customers will pay. Feedback and usability-test volunteers are welcome; please don't post private business or personal data publicly.

Link to the branch's quickstart until the pull request is merged, then use the default-branch URL. Share only in communities where the operator has permission and the post is relevant; follow each community's rules. Do not use automated bulk outreach or imply existing users, customers, endorsements, or results.

## Volunteer invitation

See the consent-aware [research kit](RESEARCH-KIT.md). Invite people to opt in, not to disclose sensitive information in a public issue. The owner should arrange any session privately, explain storage and provider policies, and obtain consent. The public call is not itself consent to participate, recording, or follow-up.

## Results post

See [Results, telemetry, and analysis](RESULTS-AND-TELEMETRY.md). Publish a results post only when actual observations exist and disclosure is authorized. Until then, the honest status is: independent tests and outcomes have not yet been recorded here.
